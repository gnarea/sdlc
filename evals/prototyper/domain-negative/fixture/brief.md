# Onboarding for new subscribers

New subscribers sign up on the website, then download the app to manage their deliveries. A quarter of them cancel before their second box, and the exit survey says that many didn't know they could skip a week. We're moving sign-up into the app, with five onboarding screens, and want to test them with people who've never used the service before we build them.

## Screens

1. **Welcome**: The service's promise ("Fresh ingredients and recipes for the week, delivered to your door"), and a button to start.
2. **Your household**: How many people the box is for (2, 3, or 4), and any dietary needs (vegetarian, no pork, no nuts, or gluten free).
3. **Your first recipes**: Twelve recipes for the first week, of which they pick three. Each recipe shows a photo, its name, and its cooking time.
4. **Delivery day**: A choice of day from Monday to Saturday, with the date of the first delivery.
5. **Your plan**: A summary of the box, its weekly price (£35 for 2 people, £45 for 3, and £55 for 4), and the date of the first delivery. It must make clear that they can skip any week, or cancel, up to five days before the delivery. The wording is still to be decided.

## Sessions

The research team has recruited eight people who've never subscribed, for one-hour sessions on our laptops. We want to learn:

- Whether they get through the five screens in under three minutes, without help.
- Whether, once they've finished, they can tell us how to skip a week.

The mock-up doesn't need to take payment or create an account.
