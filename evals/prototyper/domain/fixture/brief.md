# New checkout

Today's checkout takes five steps: basket, delivery slot, substitutions, payment, and confirmation. A third of the shoppers who reach the delivery slot step leave without paying. The new checkout puts everything on one page:

- The delivery slot, which is held for 15 minutes once chosen, with a countdown at the top of the page.
- A single setting for substitutions, which lets the picker swap an item that's out of stock for the closest match, instead of a choice of substitute for each item.
- The shopper's saved card, with one button to pay and confirm the order.

We're unsure whether shoppers will understand that their slot is only held for 15 minutes, and what the single substitutions setting will do to their order. If they don't, we'd rather find out before we build it.

Most of our shoppers are families doing their weekly shop on their phones. The research team can recruit up to ten of them for remote sessions.
