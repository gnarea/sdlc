# Planning a prototype

- Every question MUST trace to a risk, and a question whose answer wouldn't change a decision MUST be dropped.
- A technical question MUST be stated as a hypothesis, along with the result that would refute it.
- Every compromise to the system architecture, security, or privacy (e.g., leaving out authentication or encryption) MUST be listed in the plan, along with why the uncertainty around it is low enough to afford it.
