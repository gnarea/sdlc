# Credits

The agents and skills codify how I work, so nearly all of their instructions come from my own work, and the rest from the work of the people and organisations credited below. The pull request that adds or changes an instruction says where it comes from.

Some come from my work at Relaycorp, on the open source projects in [@relaycorp](https://github.com/relaycorp), [@AwalaApp](https://github.com/AwalaApp), and [@CheVeraId](https://github.com/CheVeraId), which others contributed to as well. Agents analysed their code, specs, issues, and websites (e.g., [the DDoS report](https://ddos.report/)) to find what I strive for in programming, systems architecture, and protocol design.
